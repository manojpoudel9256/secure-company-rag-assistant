# Hiring Process

## Selection steps for new graduates
Step 1: Online application with CV through the careers page.
Step 2: Online coding test (90 minutes, choose Python, JavaScript, Java or Go). Non-engineering roles take an aptitude test instead.
Step 3: Technical interview (60 minutes, online) with two engineers, discussing your projects and solving a small problem together.
Step 4: Culture and values interview (45 minutes) with a team manager and an HR member.
Step 5: Final interview (30 minutes) with a director or a co-founder, in person in Tokyo or online.
Step 6: Offer. Offers include salary, start date and team details in writing.

## Selection steps for mid-career candidates
Mid-career candidates have a CV screening, one technical or portfolio interview, a team interview and a final interview.
Senior roles may include a take-home assignment of about 3 hours.

## Timeline
The whole process usually takes 3 to 5 weeks from application to offer.
We reply to every applicant within 7 business days after each step.

## Interview language and support
Interviews can be held in English or Japanese.
Candidates can request accommodations, such as extra time for the coding test, by emailing the recruiting team.
For final interviews in Tokyo, travel costs are covered for candidates living outside the Kanto area.

## Tips from our recruiters
Prepare to explain one project you built in detail, including what you would improve.
We value curiosity, clear communication and ownership more than knowing a specific framework.
