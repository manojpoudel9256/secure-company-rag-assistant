# Products

## RouteWise
RouteWise is a delivery route planning product that uses optimization and AI to reduce driving distance and fuel use.
Customers report an average reduction of 12% in delivery distance.

## StockSense
StockSense is an inventory tracking and demand forecasting product for warehouses and factories.
It predicts demand up to 12 weeks ahead using machine learning.

## Technology stack
Our products run on AWS and use Python, Go, TypeScript, React, PostgreSQL and Kubernetes.
Machine learning models are built with PyTorch and LightGBM.
