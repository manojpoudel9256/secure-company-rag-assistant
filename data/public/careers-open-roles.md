# Open Roles at Tsubasa Cloudworks

## Currently recruiting roles and job openings
As of November 2026, Tsubasa Cloudworks is recruiting for the following roles:
Junior Backend Engineer (new graduate, April 2027 start), Tokyo.
Junior Frontend Engineer (new graduate, April 2027 start), Tokyo or Osaka.
Junior Data Analyst (new graduate, April 2027 start), Tokyo.
QA Engineer (mid-career, 2+ years of experience), Osaka.
Senior Machine Learning Engineer (mid-career, 5+ years of experience), Tokyo.
Product Designer (mid-career, 3+ years of experience), Tokyo.
Customer Success Associate (new graduate or mid-career), Tokyo, Japanese required.

## Junior Backend Engineer (new graduate)
You will build APIs and data pipelines for our delivery planning product using Python, Go and PostgreSQL.
Requirements: a bachelor's or master's degree expected by March 2027, basic programming skill in any language, and an understanding of databases and HTTP.
Nice to have: personal projects on GitHub, experience with cloud services, or internships.
Language: English required (business level); Japanese is a plus but not required.

## Junior Frontend Engineer (new graduate)
You will build web dashboards with TypeScript and React.
Requirements: a degree expected by March 2027, basic HTML, CSS and JavaScript skills, and interest in user experience.
Language: English or Japanese (conversational level in either is fine).

## Junior Data Analyst (new graduate)
You will analyse logistics data with SQL and Python and present insights to customers.
Requirements: a degree in statistics, economics, computer science or a related field expected by March 2027, and SQL basics.
Language: English required; Japanese is a plus.

## Mid-career roles
QA Engineer: test automation with Playwright and pytest, 2+ years of QA experience.
Senior Machine Learning Engineer: demand forecasting models in production, 5+ years of ML experience.
Product Designer: B2B SaaS design experience, 3+ years, with a portfolio.

## Internships
We run a paid 3-week summer internship (August) and a 2-week winter internship (February) for students.
Interns are paid 1,800 yen per hour and work on a real team project.
Internship applications open each May for summer and each November for winter.

## How to apply
Apply through the careers page at careers.tsubasa-cloudworks.example with your CV (English or Japanese).
New graduate applications for April 2027 entry close on January 31, 2027.
