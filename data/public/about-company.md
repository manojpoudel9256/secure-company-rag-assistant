# About Tsubasa Cloudworks

## Company overview
Tsubasa Cloudworks K.K. is a fictional software company created for this demo.
Tsubasa Cloudworks builds cloud software that helps logistics and manufacturing companies plan deliveries, track inventory and predict demand with AI.
The company was founded in April 2016 in Tokyo by Haruka Ishida (CEO) and Daniel Moreau (CTO).
Our mission is to make supply chains calmer, smarter and greener.

## Number of employees
As of October 2026, Tsubasa Cloudworks has 238 employees.
About 140 employees work in engineering, data and design.
About 98 employees work in sales, customer success, HR, finance and operations.
Employees come from 19 countries, and about 22% of employees are non-Japanese nationals.
The average age of employees is 33 years.

## Offices
Our headquarters is in Shibuya, Tokyo (Shibuya Sky Garden Building, 12F).
We also have a branch office in Umeda, Osaka, with about 45 employees.
Most teams work in a hybrid style, combining office days and remote days.

## Business and customers
Tsubasa Cloudworks serves more than 320 business customers in Japan and Southeast Asia.
Annual revenue for fiscal year 2025 was 3.1 billion yen.
The company is privately held and backed by venture capital investors.

## Working languages
The main working language in engineering teams is English.
Sales and customer success teams mainly work in Japanese.
Company-wide announcements are published in both Japanese and English.
