# Candidate FAQ

## Do I need to speak Japanese?
Japanese is not required for most engineering roles; English is the main language in engineering teams.
Japanese is required for the Customer Success Associate role.

## Do you hire international students and overseas applicants?
Yes. International students graduating from Japanese or overseas universities are welcome to apply.
We sponsor work visas for successful candidates.

## Can I apply if my degree is not in computer science?
Yes. About 30% of our new graduate engineers studied other subjects, such as physics, economics or mechanical engineering.
We look for programming fundamentals and the ability to learn.

## Is there a dress code?
There is no strict dress code. Business casual is recommended for customer meetings.

## Can I choose my team?
New graduates finish training first and then are assigned to a team based on their interests and team needs.

## Can I reapply if I am not selected?
Yes. You can reapply after 6 months.

## What is the probation period?
The probation period is 3 months, with the same salary and benefits.
