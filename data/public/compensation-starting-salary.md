# Compensation and Starting Salary

## New graduate starting salary
The starting salary for new graduate (junior) engineers is 4,800,000 yen per year.
This is paid as a monthly salary of 350,000 yen plus a performance bonus of about 600,000 yen per year.
The starting salary for new graduate engineers with a master's degree is 5,100,000 yen per year.
The starting salary for the Junior Data Analyst role is 4,500,000 yen per year.
The starting salary for the Customer Success Associate role is 4,000,000 yen per year.
The monthly salary includes fixed overtime pay for up to 20 hours; overtime beyond 20 hours is paid separately.

## Salary ranges for mid-career roles
QA Engineer: 5,500,000 to 7,500,000 yen per year.
Senior Machine Learning Engineer: 9,000,000 to 13,000,000 yen per year.
Product Designer: 6,500,000 to 9,000,000 yen per year.
The final offer depends on skills and experience shown during the interviews.

## Salary reviews and promotion
Salaries are reviewed twice a year, in April and October.
Promotions are based on a published career ladder with clear expectations for each level.
New graduates usually reach the mid-level engineer grade in 2 to 3 years.

## Bonus
The performance bonus is paid once a year in December, based on company and individual results.
