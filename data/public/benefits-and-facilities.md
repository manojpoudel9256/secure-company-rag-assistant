# Benefits and Facilities

## Working style and hours
Tsubasa Cloudworks uses a hybrid working style: 2 office days per week for most teams, and the rest can be remote.
Core hours are 11:00 to 15:00 with flextime around them.
Standard working hours are 8 hours per day, 5 days a week (Monday to Friday).

## Holidays and leave
Employees get weekends, Japanese national holidays and summer and new-year holidays (about 125 days off per year).
Paid annual leave starts at 12 days in the first year and increases with years of service.
We also offer 3 days of special refresh leave per year, plus maternity, paternity and caregiver leave.

## Insurance and allowances
All employees are enrolled in Japanese social insurance (health insurance, pension, employment insurance).
Commuting costs are covered up to 50,000 yen per month.
A remote work allowance of 5,000 yen per month is paid to cover internet and electricity.
A housing allowance of 20,000 yen per month is paid to employees living within 3 km of an office.

## Learning and growth
Every employee has a learning budget of 150,000 yen per year for books, courses, conferences and certifications.
New graduates join a 3-month training program covering engineering basics, our products and business skills.
Each new employee is assigned a mentor for the first 6 months.
Japanese and English language lessons are free for all employees.

## Office facilities
Both offices have free drinks and snacks, standing desks and quiet focus rooms.
The Tokyo office has a cafe area with free lunch on Wednesdays and a small library.
Employees receive a laptop (MacBook Pro or Windows) and a 27-inch monitor for home use.

## Support for international employees
We sponsor work visas for overseas hires.
We help international employees with relocation, including a one-time relocation support payment of up to 300,000 yen and help finding housing.
